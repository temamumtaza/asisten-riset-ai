# Asisten Riset AI design direction

## Design Read

Reading this as: a research desk for Indonesian university students, with an editorial field-notes language, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1.

This direction is derived from the product purpose and the requested high-quality interface. It is not a claim that a visual brand was supplied separately.

## Identity

The interface is a quiet research desk. It makes the chain from question to source to supervisor feedback visible without turning research into a form wizard.

The identity motif is the margin rule: a thin vertical line used to connect a research question, evidence, and the next action. It is specific to traceable research and replaces decorative technology motifs.

## Palette

- Canvas: #f2efe7, a paper-like base that supports long reading sessions.
- Surface: #fbfaf6, reserved for the active work surface.
- Ink: #1e2b2f, chosen for readable high-contrast text.
- Moss: #536b52, used for confirmed and completed states.
- Evidence blue: #245a73, used only for paper provenance and links.
- Review rust: #a64a3b, the single attention accent for work that needs a decision.
- Rule: #d5cec1, used for structure rather than decoration.

The active palette is two core colors plus one semantic set and one accent. Status never relies on color alone.

## Typography

- Display: Newsreader, because its editorial forms signal close reading and make research notes feel authored.
- UI: DM Sans, because its open shapes stay clear at compact sizes for search results, controls, and metadata.
- The fallback stack stays readable when a font cannot load.

## Composition

- Workspace desktop order: question, evidence, next action.
- Search results are reference sheets, not equal feature cards.
- Supervisor feedback stays next to the item being reviewed.
- Spacing varies by reading depth. Large gaps separate research phases; compact groups contain actions.
- Cards are used only where a boundary helps a decision. The active evidence surface gets the only stronger elevation.

## Motion

MOTION 1 means hover, focus, and 160 to 220 ms state transitions only. Motion exists to confirm state changes and preserve orientation, not to decorate an empty screen.

## Copy

Use plain Indonesian. Prefer concrete actions such as “Cari paper pertama”, “Simpan kutipan”, “Minta masukan dosen”, and “Tandai sudah ditindaklanjuti”. Avoid emoji, em dashes, fake metrics, generic AI claims, and unexplained technical words in the product UI.
